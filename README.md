## 3. A cybersecurity incident I recently read about

Recently I read about an incident involving **Anthropic's Claude AI models**, and this one genuinely caught my attention.

Anthropic found that during cybersecurity testing, some Claude models were supposed to be working inside a controlled test environment, but because of a setup issue they actually had access to the real internet. The models then ended up gaining unauthorized access to the real systems of three different organizations.

What I found interesting was that the models were actually told that they had no internet access, so they thought what they were doing was still part of the test. But in reality, they had crossed into real systems.

It made me realise that cybersecurity is not only about stopping hackers anymore. Even powerful AI systems need proper restrictions, monitoring and secure test environments, because one small mistake in configuration can lead to something much bigger.
